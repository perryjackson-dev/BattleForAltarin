# Battle for Altarin — locked design decisions

- Title: Battle for Altarin
- Original grounded medieval low-fantasy setting; not Arthurian; no futuristic technology
- Browser-based strategy game with City, Field, and World views
- Buildings have maximum level 10 and exactly 3 visual ranks across those levels
- Visual rank is derived from level: 1–3 Rank I, 4–7 Rank II, 8–10 Rank III
- Fixed building plots rather than free placement
- Planned core resources: Food, Wood, Stone, Ore, Gold
- Population system, construction timers, troop training, Council/Commander system, research, and PvE world-map combat
- PvP and alliances are not part of the initial Alpha
- The Alpha currently has six fixed City plots and permits simultaneous construction on separate plots.
